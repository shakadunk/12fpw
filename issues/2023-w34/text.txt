I went on a first & last date with someone a couple of weeks ago who, after sharing some bad calamari and an odd combination of drinks, was prompted by the We’re Not Really Strangers cards they brought along to describe me in three words. “Pragmatic, minimalist, and cold”, they said, to which I responded, “Sounds like you’re describing a MacBook, but okay.” I wouldn’t refute someone’s opinion, and I don’t necessarily think these are unattractive qualities (if you like a Lydia Tár type, for instance, or have an approval kink), but I wouldn’t have used any of those to describe myself. It made for an interesting interaction and conversation, but, ultimately, I can’t give their words too much weight, especially since the poetry they shared with me wasn’t exactly exceptional.

I’ve been thinking about furniture (as usual), using my building’s gym more, plums not being as good as I remember from childhood, and fall creeping in.

With a Reserved Warmth,
SD
