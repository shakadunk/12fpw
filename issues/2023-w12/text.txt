It’s the twelfth week of these frames. Only forty to go and the first phase of this experiment will be complete. I’m thinking about printing a zine of all the frames from Year 1, but am also thinking about not thinking about any of this more than one week out.

As an aside, I am always attracted to words with four or more consonants in a row (like twelfth), for whatever that’s worth.

The sun is back outside and I am thinking about Johann Wolfgang von Goethe eschewing science and physics to write a book on color theory based purely on vibes, Girbaud jeans, switching over to cold brew season, La Croix Limoncello being back on shelves and me being happily addicted once more, healthy detachment & accepting the impermanence of relationships and friendships (call that zenships), the Simone Leigh show coming to the ICA Boston, Florida’s increasingly-insane legislation, Danielle Levitt’s We Are Experienced photography series, I have a lot of traveling to do this fall, and coining Aspirational Asceticism as a lifestyle.

Naturally Essenced,
SD
