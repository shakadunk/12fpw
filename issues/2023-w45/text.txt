I’ve been thinking and talking about the percolating Great Reverse Migration of young people, especially Black, reversing the migration that our grand and great-grand parents made out of the rural south to New York, Chicago, Detroit, Philly, etc.. LA is a vapid industry-town and NY is absolutely cooked squalor, so going back to these places for reasonable costs of living and consolidated state-level voting power is not a bad idea.

Any time I’m in a new city, I like to know where the Apple Store is, because that’s most likely the area I want to stay or hang out in (If it doesn't have an Apple Store, it’s not a real city–I don’t make the rules). I think, with a remote job, all I’d need is an Apple Store, a nice third-wave coffee shop, and decent sushi within a 30 minute drive, and I could probably live anywhere. Make the South Sexy Again.
