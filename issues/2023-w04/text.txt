*“Desire is not simple or safe. In life and in art, desire is the complication.”*

The way letterforms can be abstracted endlessly while still conveying different layers of meaning is, I think, what has drawn me to graffiti my entire life, as a sort of conceptual, linguistic, political art practice. I say that to say, this is probably what predisposed me to Cy Twombly’s paintings. The first time I saw one, almost certainly as a small jpeg on my Tumblr dashboard about a decade ago, although I had no context for and didn’t “get” it, I knew I felt something immediate. I say that to say, his paintings, like graffiti, like rap, like theater, are, for me, about movement, myth, motion, poetry, rhythm. Language is just symbols, and symbols are a language of whatever you want them to be. I say all of this to say I finally started reading the Cy Twombly biography and it is great.

In Motion,
SD
