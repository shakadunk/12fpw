There has been a resurgence, I think, of guerrilla poetry readings as social events. This is mildly interesting in a kind of “sign of the times” way, but most poetry is bad, so these events are likely way less engaging in practice than in theory. That being said, I was recently asked to read a text-based poster I created for an artist project in collaboration with Boston Art Review at their year-end celebration and it went very well. Maybe I think of these images as poems. Anyways, the poster was blue and white and so are these frames, more or less.
