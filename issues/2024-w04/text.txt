I’m in Orlando and thinking about space and time because of it. Since I left over a decade ago, so much has changed in the city that I feel equally as alienated as I do familiar. I went to a little third wave coffee shop, which seems to be where the hip young people go, and crossed paths with the very first girl I ever had a crush on in the first grade––a wild coincidence. Being able to say, “I haven’t seen you in about 25 years” is such an old-head thing to say that I almost tripped over it as it came out of my mouth––a wild inevitability.

In other news, I’m working diligently on getting Vol. I (2023) of these Frames printed and set on a coffee table near you. If you are reading this and have experience or connections with printing/zines/publishing, I’m all ears.

With the same face I had in 1999, just hairier,
SD
