I just finished reading [Darkly: Black History and America’s Gothic Soul](https://www.google.com/search?client=safari&ie=UTF-8&oe=UTF-8&q=darkly%20leila%20taylor&rls=en), and it left me with sense of excitement about 2024. Not necessarily because of the book’s contents, but because, in exploring what “goth(ic)” means, broadly and relative to a specifically Black experience, she talks a lot about possibility.

Four or so years into a decade–about halfway through–is when defining things for that decade begin to take shape. A new year is simply a clear marker of a new time, which, as an idea, excites me. The many possibilities. If the results are good, that’s great, if they’re bad, there’s something spectacular about watching a thing become a ruin with your very own eyes. Either way, the memes are going to be great.
