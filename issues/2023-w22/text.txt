I often think about how artists of all sorts tend to do “lighter” but more masterful work in their later, more developed years. In consumer arts like music and fashion, people tend to conflate this with laziness, or being basic, or washed, but, as someone creatively preoccupied with creating the greatest effect with the least interference, it’s often my favorite kind of work. I think immediately about Jay Z’s performance on 4:44 and Picasso’s bull sketches (below) or self-portraits.
