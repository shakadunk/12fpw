Replaces the old Mailchimp-based plan. The site and the email come from the same post data.
Repo: github.com/shakadunk/12fpw. Domain: 12fpw.com (currently serving the Mailchimp archive).

## Design (new)
- Pure black (#000) background, white text, full-color images floating on black. Light/dark toggle kept as a secondary option.
- Each post = "Week N YEAR" heading, text, then exactly 12 images. The hero is always image 1. The small underline that used to sit under the heading was removed on 2026-10-27 (cleaner without it). The year is in grey and is the same font size as "Week N" (it used to be smaller).
- NO HOVER TOOLTIP on images (2026-10-28, Shaka's request): the links used to carry a title ("Search for this image on Google", or "Open link" on custom links) that browsers showed when the cursor rested on an image; the titles were removed everywhere. The links themselves are unchanged.
- Every image is a link: clicking it opens a Google Lens reverse-image search of that image (`lens.google.com/uploadbyurl?url=<the image's public address>`) in a new tab, so viewers can trace where it came from without Shaka finding each source by hand (decision 2026-10-27). It needs the site's public address, which the build takes from `siteUrl`, `SITE_URL` or Vercel's production URL; with none, no links are drawn. Lens can only fetch the image once the site is live, and it follows the address the site is deployed at (12fpw.vercel.app now, 12fpw.com after the move). Source links Shaka tracked down by hand for the old posts are not carried over.

## Email
- Colorless on purpose: just text and images, no background or text colors set, so it follows each reader's light/dark setting.
- A black PNG logo would vanish in dark mode, so use live text for the name in the email, or switch to a new logo. The website now uses a plain-text name, so the same plain text works in the email too.
- Footer keeps the postal address and an unsubscribe link (both legally required).

## Process
1. Shaka submits raw text + 12 images on a private, password-protected upload page on the site.
2. The pipeline builds the post, publishes a preview, and sends a test email to Shaka.
3. Shaka approves. Nothing reaches subscribers before that.
4. On approval the post goes live and the email goes to subscribers.
5. The email uses the site's hosted image URLs. No Mailchimp.

## Sending
- Expected list: under 100 subscribers.
- Resend free plan: 3,000 emails/month, 100/day cap, can also receive email. Check audience and contact limits at setup.
- Past 100 subscribers: upgrade or switch (Brevo free is 300/day with its branding; Amazon SES is about $0.16 per 1,000). Keep the sending code provider-agnostic.
- Sending from 12fpw.com needs SPF/DKIM records.
- The signup form (currently hidden everywhere) adds people to the provider's list; the provider handles unsubscribe links.

## Scroll-up bar (replaces the earlier VIEW / TOP archive bar; 2026-10-26)
- On every page (home feed, archive, week pages): scrolling down hides the header and nothing replaces it; scrolling back up slides in ONE frosted band across the whole top of the screen. It is not shown while the header is still on screen. Hide delay: scrolling down does not hide it at once; it lingers first, for about 1.4 seconds on a phone (under 720px wide) and about 1.8 seconds on larger screens (changed 2026-10-28 from 1 second everywhere at Shaka's request: a little longer, and a bit longer again on desktop; the two numbers are hideDelay() in app.js). Scrolling back up during that time cancels the countdown (the bar stays), a fresh scroll down starts a fresh countdown, and continuous scrolling down does not keep pushing the hide back. While a mouse rests on the bar or its menu the bar stays; the countdown starts again when the mouse leaves (only if the bar was on its way out). If the menu is open it stays open during the delay and closes when the bar hides. Reaching the top of the page hides it immediately, because the header is back on screen.
- HINT (decided 2026-10-27 after Shaka tested on his iPhone): the FIRST time you scroll down from the top of a page, the bar slides in, stays for the same time (1.4s phone, 1.8s larger screens), then slides away, so people learn it exists before it disappears. It happens once per page load (going back to the top and scrolling down again does not repeat it; opening another page or reloading does), and after that the bar only appears on scroll up. It uses the normal hide delay, so scrolling up during that time keeps it, like always.
- Layout (every screen size since 2026-10-28): LIGHT/DARK left, TOP in the centre (where the header name sits; the name is not repeated in the bar), ARCHIVE right, matching the header. The name in the header is the link home.
- TAP HANDLING (2026-10-28, after Shaka reported on his iPhone that ARCHIVE in the scroll-up bar needed TWO taps while LIGHT/DARK and TOP worked on the first): could NOT be reproduced in the test browser (no iPhone or WebKit available here), so the fix is defensive. On touch and pen the ARCHIVE menu now toggles on the finger lifting (pointerup) instead of waiting for the browser's synthesized click, which iPhones can swallow; the click that follows is ignored, a drag or a release off the button does nothing, a mouse still uses click (with the hover logic) and the keyboard still works. All buttons also got `touch-action: manipulation`. Tests: tests/test_touchtap.py. If the double tap still happens on the phone, ask: does it happen only right after scrolling up (while the page is still settling) or also after waiting a few seconds, and does the menu flash open and shut? Next suspects: iOS swallowing the first tap while momentum scrolling is still stopping, or a paint problem with the menu's frosted mask.
- ARCHIVE in the bar drops down GRID / CALENDAR (current view in full white, the others dimmed), the same menu as in the header, including hover-to-open on desktop.
- Look: the band is about 26% tint (black in the dark theme, white in the light theme) with a 14px blur, feathered along its bottom edge so it dissolves into the photo; about 37px tall plus the feather. The frosting reaches 12px below the bar's own box so the text sits balanced: about 13px from the top of the screen to the tops of the capitals and about 13px from their bottoms to where the frosting visibly ends (checked by tests/test_balance.py, within 1px, both themes, phone and desktop; before 2026-10-27 it reached only 8px and the letters looked low). The band keeps this single depth at all times. When the ARCHIVE menu is open, the bar and the menu are ONE continuous frosted element: the bar's own layer is extended downward under the menu, with a mask that is the band unioned with a soft ROUNDED shape under the menu, so there is a single blur and a single tint and no overlap (the same 26% tint and 14px blur in both themes). The rounded shape has bottom corners of radius 40px and edges that fade in over 24px; its outer edges sit 41px to the left of the labels, 26px right of the menu and 40px below it (more room on the left was requested; the left edge used to sit right against the text; on 2026-10-28 the tint went from 30% to 26% and the room below went from 30px to 40px: under the last item the tint now stays at full strength about 33px below the letters on a phone, up from 23px, before the fade starts, because the old gap looked tight to the eye even though it measured fine). It is drawn by app.js as one smooth image each time the menu opens (so there are no joins or hairline seams; an earlier attempt that built the curve from several gradient pieces left faint lines at the joins). History: an even earlier version used a separate patch layer behind the menu; where it overlapped the bar it made a denser stripe (about 11 grey levels over the band's tint) that read as a separate box with a gap, so it was merged on 2026-10-26. Browsers without CSS mask compositing fall back to the separate patch. The menu has no solid panel of its own.
